Lodestone · camp action · design pitch

Hunting: four ways to bring home meat

Each one is a short, repeatable game you get better at, sits beside fishing as a camp action (and a practice mode on the launch menu), and leaves room for gear upgrades, the Hunter, Doggo and the dice. Sketches are graybox. Numbers come later.

Your idea, developed

A · Bow Blind

Duck Hunt in a forest glade, with a bow that fights back.

How it plays

  • A small open forest map built from our tiles, trees and bushes. The hero crouches behind a log. The mouse is the aim.
  • Animals break from one bush and run for another. Some stop halfway to look around: that pause is the easy shot.
  • Hold to draw. The ring tightens to a sweet band, then the aim starts to shake. Let go in the band for a straight, fast arrow. Too early and it drops short.
  • Arrows take time to fly, so you lead fast animals.
  • Stealth: sit still and the noise pips drain, animals come out sooner and pause longer. Sweep the bow around fast and they bolt.
  • A clean hit is a catch. A graze leaves a wounded animal that runs off. A miss spooks it. Five arrows; the glade holds three animals a trip, like the fishing pond.
You improve at
reading which bush is next, leading shots, timing the draw, knowing when to wait.
Upgrades
bow tiers (wider sweet band, faster draw), bigger quiver, broadheads (a graze still counts), a cloak that makes less noise.
Hunter
Steady Aim: no shake while still. Sees the next bush's rustle earlier.
Doggo
SNIFF marks the bush holding the next animal. FETCH runs down a wounded one before it's lost.
Dice
the party's hunt roll on entering sets how rich the glade is: how many animals, how bold.
New

B · Stalk

Red light, green light with a deer, then one shot.

How it plays

  • A small top-down meadow, walked with WASD like the explore maps. Hoof prints lead to a grazing animal.
  • Move only while its head is down. When it lifts its head, freeze. Its sight cone shows; tall grass hides you.
  • Wind blows across the map. Come at it downwind or it smells you.
  • Reach the shot ring and you get one shot, using the same draw as A. Closer is easier.
You improve at
reading the tells (a tail flick before the head comes up), picking a route through cover, playing the wind.
Upgrades
soft boots (faster crouch), scent oil (wind matters less), a longer-range bow.
Hunter
gets an ear-twitch warning half a second before the head comes up.
Doggo
SNIFF shows faded tracks. Send him wide to circle round and push the deer toward you.
Dice
step on a twig: a nerve check. Fail and the deer looks up.
New

C · Snare Line

A tiny planning puzzle. No reflexes, all knowledge.

How it plays

  • At camp you see the ground around it, with animal runs drawn as faint tracks.
  • Place three snares and an optional bait. Then the night plays out fast: animals walk their runs, and you watch what trips.
  • Each animal has habits: rabbits hug cover, foxes avoid bait you handled, boars snap thin wire.
You improve at
learning each animal's habits. A Lodestone page could record them as you discover them.
Upgrades
more snares, wire snares for boars, baits from foraged berries and herbs.
Hunter
sees every run (others see half), and his snares hold better. This is the "trapping" half of your Hunter idea.
Doggo
SNIFF shows which runs are fresh tonight. Downside: a dog at camp scares off foxes.
Dice
a snare holds or breaks on a roll against the animal's strength.
New

D · Corgi Drive

A herding game where you are the dog.

How it plays

  • WASD runs Doggo through a meadow. Animals flee from him as a loose flock.
  • BARK (Space) pushes them in a cone, with a few barks a round. Press too hard and the flock scatters.
  • Drive them through the gold gap between the trees. The hero waits there and shoots what passes, on a timing tap.
You improve at
herding: angles, pacing, saving barks for the stragglers.
Upgrades
Doggo's own levels: speed, bark range, an extra bark.
Hunter
shoots from the gap automatically and never misses a bird that passes.
Doggo
he is the game. Without him in the party there is no drive, so this works best as Doggo's own way to hunt, not the only one.
Dice
a tame check for skittish herds (the oxen-calming skill from your pitch fits here).

Recommendation

Build A, your Bow Blind, as the hunt. It's the most repeatable of the four, it grows with practice, and every hook lands on it: bow upgrades, the Hunter's Steady Aim, Doggo's SNIFF and FETCH, and the hunt roll. It also reuses the fishing overlay, so it's the quickest to get in your hands.

Borrow two ideas into it. From B: animals that pause and look up, so sitting still is the stealth. From D: a FLUSH command, where Doggo charges the bush you pick so you choose when the animal breaks.

Keep C for later as a second camp action, TRAP. It's the low-reflex choice for players who'd rather plan, and it fits "same as last night" well.

Where it lives: the camp's HUNT button opens the glade the way FISH opens the pond, "same as last night" still uses the quick dice roll, and the launch menu gets a practice glade. Raw meat goes in the pack as rations, like fish.
