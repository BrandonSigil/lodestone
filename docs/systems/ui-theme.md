- [USER] 2026-10-30 (menu-style-go): "lets build the menus in-engine so we can more easily animate them and stuff." Brandon then picked "frame green", direction A, from his own Picsart run (`art/picsart/out/hand/menu_style_brandon.png`). `FramedBox` draws it in code: outside in, an ink outline, a silver band with a light top edge, a blue band with a dark lower edge, a dark line, a cream line, an ink line, then deep green #064e3c with a soft mottle (a noise texture made once). The inner lines notch inward round a blue gem in a silver mount at each corner, and two olive leaves come out of each outer corner. `pill` makes a button (round ends, a gem with leaves each side at both ends), `pointer` a V under a tooltip, and `tint`/`glow` the hover, pressed and greyed looks. Bands scale with the box (`REF`); ornaments shrink less, so small buttons keep their gems. The palette constants in `UiTheme` follow his colours; `UiTheme.frame/plate/diamond` serve screens that paint themselves, and `pop_in`/`pop_out`/`fade_in` open and close menus. If code leaves or gems ever need richer painting, the art runner can cut sprites (corner leaf-and-gem, button end-cap, pointer).
- `UiTheme.make()` (`game/scripts/ui/ui_theme.gd`) builds the one shared Theme. `Game._ready` merges it into the engine's default theme, which every Control without its own theme falls back to. (A theme on the root window doesn't reach Controls under a CanvasLayer, which is where all the HUDs live.)
- Use these instead of hand-made styles: `theme_type_variation` `DialogPanel` (dialogues, the Lodestone menu, PACK, the dock's bar and portrait panel), `MenuPanel` (small menus, the dock's pop-up columns), `InfoPanel` (read-outs; no ornaments), `TipPanel` (the fight's corner details panel), `TitlePlate` (the long thin plate a menu title sits on; `AdventureMenu` puts its titles on it), and for Labels `MenuTitle` and `SubTitle`. Buttons need nothing: they're pills. Godot's own tooltips get the V pointer.
- On it now: explore and overworld dialogues (they pop open and fade shut), the Lodestone menu, PACK, the options screen, the character sheet (draws with `UiTheme.frame/plate/diamond`, fades in), and the fight's menu, forecast and action layouts.

- Art (2026-10-30): Brandon approved the painted pieces of his look, cut by the art runner into `game/art/source/ui/menu/look_panel.png`, `look_title_plate.png`, `look_button.png` and `look_tooltip.png`. `UiTheme.look()` scales a piece to game size (panels 0.3 to 0.45, the pill 0.24, the plate 0.3) and nine-slices it on its corners: the panel's leaves and gem reach about 105 px into the piece, so its margin is 110; the pill and the plate keep their 85 to 88 px ends whole and stretch, never tile, vertically. ## Windows that fit, and the victory and level-up screens (2026-11-01)

[USER] 2026-11-01 playtest: windows "either too small or too large", "way too much text", "Menus spill out of the window", "Look at battle victory menus and beef up this report". So:

- **Fit**: `UiTheme.keep_inside(box, area, base, top)` keeps a box in the play area: centred across, at the top or standing on the frame, scaled down from `base` whenever it would spill over, and refit each time the box changes size. Explore (`_new_dialog`, area `_dialog_area`: above the frame's crest) and overworld (`_new_dialog`) use it for every box. `pop_in` grows to the fitted size (meta `fit_k`).
- **Victory screen** (`VictoryUi`, `game/scripts/ui/victory_ui.gd`): a dimmed screen with the WIN plate; icon chips for XP each (`star_guide`), foes down (`knock_out`), coins (`gold`) and every loot item (its own icon, else `sack`); a card per piece with its portrait (or pawn), LV, an XP bar that fills and rolls over into a LV UP plate with the stats gained as icons (`STAT_ICON`: HP potion, damage strength, armor shield, magic rare, crit focus), a NEW TRICK gem when a pick waits, HP (potion) and heart (`heal`) bars with the heart change; bonds that grew as face, heart, face, LV. A key or click fills every bar at once; the next one presses the first button. Hosts take `VictoryUi.snapshot` before `Game.fight_morale` and `Game.award_xp`, then `VictoryUi.report`. Explore fights and road fights (`combat_scene.gd`) use it; a lost fight keeps the plain box. Shots: `explore_shot.gd` steps `win`, `win_lv`, `win_mid`.
- **Level-up screen**: [USER] "Dog level 2 window isnt needed. Make that the heading of the skill pick menu". A member that levelled gets a box only when a pick waits with something to pick (`LevelUpUi.has_pick`): `LevelUpUi.header` (LV UP plate, new level, name, stats gained as icons), then `picks`, then Decide later. A level with no pick shows only on the victory screen. A pick with nothing real to choose (empty or all locked) no longer shows an empty row and a greyed button.
- **Lodestone menu** (`AdventureMenu.lodestone`): one card per member (face, name, LV, the hero's job and its level, XP / HP / heart bars with the band on the heart bar and its effect as a tooltip, damage / armor / crit / magic as icons). A member with a pick or a job branch waiting gets a NEW TRICK button that opens it under the cards (one member at a time, pressed again closes); blessings, setbacks and the meal as chips; bonds as face-heart-face chips with a bar to the next level and TALK at home; the buttons two by two. `UiBar` is the shared rounded bar.
