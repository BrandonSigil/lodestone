# Menu theme

[USER] 2026-10-30: every menu looks consistent (the Lodestone menu, character sheets, the pack, options, dialogues, the world map UI), with variety where needed.

- [USER] 2026-10-30 (menu-style-go): "lets build the menus in-engine so we can more easily animate them and stuff." Brandon then picked "frame green", direction A, from his own Picsart run (`art/picsart/out/hand/menu_style_brandon.png`). `FramedBox` draws it in code: outside in, an ink outline, a silver band with a light top edge, a blue band with a dark lower edge, a dark line, a cream line, an ink line, then deep green #064e3c with a soft mottle (a noise texture made once). The inner lines notch inward round a blue gem in a silver mount at each corner, and two olive leaves come out of each outer corner. `pill` makes a button (round ends, a gem with leaves each side at both ends), `pointer` a V under a tooltip, and `tint`/`glow` the hover, pressed and greyed looks. Bands scale with the box (`REF`); ornaments shrink less, so small buttons keep their gems. The palette constants in `UiTheme` follow his colours; `UiTheme.frame/plate/diamond` serve screens that paint themselves, and `pop_in`/`pop_out`/`fade_in` open and close menus. If code leaves or gems ever need richer painting, the art runner can cut sprites (corner leaf-and-gem, button end-cap, pointer).
- `UiTheme.make()` (`game/scripts/ui/ui_theme.gd`) builds the one shared Theme. `Game._ready` merges it into the engine's default theme, which every Control without its own theme falls back to. (A theme on the root window doesn't reach Controls under a CanvasLayer, which is where all the HUDs live.)
- Use these instead of hand-made styles: `theme_type_variation` `DialogPanel` (dialogues, the Lodestone menu, PACK, the dock's bar and portrait panel), `MenuPanel` (small menus, the dock's pop-up columns), `InfoPanel` (read-outs; no ornaments), `TipPanel` (the fight's corner details panel), `TitlePlate` (the long thin plate a menu title sits on; `AdventureMenu` puts its titles on it), and for Labels `MenuTitle` and `SubTitle`. Buttons need nothing: they're pills. Godot's own tooltips get the V pointer.
- On it now: explore and overworld dialogues (they pop open and fade shut), the Lodestone menu, PACK, the options screen, the character sheet (draws with `UiTheme.frame/plate/diamond`, fades in), and the fight's menu, forecast and action layouts.
