# Overworld and the E2E loop

The first playable loop ([USER] 2026-10-28, `docs/decisions.md` "End-to-end loop"). Placeholder art throughout.

## The loop
1. **New game** (launch menu) wipes the save and starts in the hero's cottage. The hero is alone, a *nobody* (class `nobody`).
2. **The village** (`village`, no fog): take a weapon from the smithy rack (sword, daggers or bow, and the book once it's found in the house by the square; `data/weapons.csv` says what each adds and which job it makes), pick up a rope (smithy yard barrel), food (tavern barrel) and potions (the chest in the cottage).
3. **The dog**: the west home on the river ridge is locked (`door_dog`: a hard BASH). Its window on the right front wall (`dog_window`) climbs into `house_dog`; LET DOG OUT sets `met_dog` and the dog joins the party. Once he is out the front door opens both ways.
4. **The statue** in the square (placeholder blob `ph_statue`) holds the glowing Lodestone; TAKE sets `has_lodestone` and a LODESTONE button appears above the bottom frame, right side. The gate only lets you out once you have it (`village_gate`, needs `flag:has_lodestone`).
5. **The overworld** (`scenes/overworld.tscn`, see below): the hex world map. From Home walk straight up to the highlands, which hold the snowy pass; forest roads (left) and farmland (right) are open too, and the other tiles show, shut.
6. **The snowy pass** (`snow_pass`, built by `tools/explore/build_snow_pass.py`, then dressed to the painted look's rules by `tools/dress_explore.gd`: tidy edges, three pillars, a beige road from the foot to the crypt stair, one-cell stair flights): five terraces from level 0 to 8, two wolves low down, a bandit pair holding the last stair, a cache, a hidden find in the snow, a high ledge only the rope reaches (`rope_climb`, needs `item:rope`), and the wolf den (`wolf_den`: a pair of wolves and a lone one further in, bones to rummage).
7. **The crypt** (`crypt`) through the stair at the top of the pass: the Lich King (class `lich`, a placeholder piece) waits on the throne terrace. His group has `boss` (more XP) and `end`: beating him sets `e2e_done` and shows TEST DONE. A beaten boss stays beaten for good (flag `beaten_<map>_<group>`, `ExploreRules.stays_beaten`): falls and new trips in bring every other group back, not him.
8. **Falling costs nothing** ([USER] 2026-10-28): when the party falls, everyone wakes in the village square (`Game.SQUARE`) at full HP with every item, level and flag. The first fall sets `lodestone_revealed`.

## The overworld: the hex world map
- **Tiles** ([USER] 2026-11-01, card pm-world-paths): Brandon's twelve explorable tiles from `art/templates/world/hex_world_guide.png`, numbered as on the card: 1 Home (`town`), 2 forest roads, 3 coastal beach, 4 frozen dam, 5 highlands (`pass`), 6 dwarven mines, 7 snowy plains, 8 farmland, 9 grassland, 10 and 12 enchanted woods, 11 capital city. `data/overworld_nodes.csv`: `num`, `kind` (the glyph and tint), `col`, `row`, `height` (the guide's grid: flat-top hexes, even columns half a row lower), `open`, the explore `map` and entry cell, `maps` that belong to the tile so leaving them lands there, `clear_flag`, `after` (a tile that opens only once that one is cleared) and `weather`.
- **Free routes:** any tile leads on to the tiles it shares an edge with (`TravelRules.neighbours`); each shared edge is a leg in `data/overworld_paths.csv` (`seconds`, `enc_min`/`enc_max`, `strangeness`, the `art_set` road fights use). Legs run both ways, in a straight line from top to top (`TravelRules.path_between`). A test checks the legs are exactly the shared edges.
- **Open now:** Home, forest roads (left), highlands (straight) and farmland (right). Highlands is the old pass: its map is the snowy pass, with the wolf den and the crypt, so the Lich loop is unchanged ([AI], Brandon can change it). Forest roads and farmland name their maps (`forest_roads`, `farmland`, the map builder's graybox maps); until a map file exists, arriving says MAP NOT MADE YET and there is no GO IN (`map_ready`). The dwarven mines past the highlands open once the Lich King falls (`after: pass`), in place of the old FAR PLACE; nothing is there yet. Every other tile shows, shut, with a "?".
- **Drawing** (code, flat graybox, art later): each tile is a raised hex block on the 2:1 camera, taller where `height` is higher (`TravelRules.tile_top`, `tile_pos`), drawn back to front on the guide's cream. An open tile's top takes a light wash of its land's colour (`KIND_TINT`) and a small glyph; a shut one is darker grey. Roads are dotted lines on every edge whose two tiles are open now. The view fits the tiles' bounds into the room above the frame, left of the side box. A click picks the tile whose top face is under it. Cleared tiles wear the gold seal, tiles with an elite still standing the red "!".
- **The painted map** ([USER] 2026-11-01, pm-dev-painted-world-map): when every tile has an outline in `data/world_tiles.csv`, the overworld draws Brandon's picture (`art/source/world/world_map_v1.png`, row `_map` of that table) as its ground, fitted into the room above the frame, instead of the graybox blocks. Each tile's `outline` is its border traced by eye from the picture, in picture pixels, clockwise from the top; where the painted border fades, the outline closes along the coast. A better map later needs only a new `_map` path and new outlines. The tile's centre is the outline's centre of area (`TravelRules.centre`); the party stands there and roads run centre to centre. Two tiles are neighbours when their outlines share two or more corners (`TravelRules.share_border`, 6 px), and `overworld_paths.csv` has exactly one leg per shared border (checked by `test_hex_world_map`). Matching the painting moved three legs: beach-highlands, mines-back woods and grassland-city are gone; forest roads-mines, snowy plains-grassland and farmland-woods are new.
- **Fog** on the painted map: a tile not seen yet is covered by parchment with soft drifting clouds (a noise texture made in code, clipped to the outline). Standing on a tile sees it and every neighbour, for good (meta flags `seen_tile_<id>`, `TravelRules.discover`). Fogged tiles can't be clicked. A seen tile that is shut for now has a light dark wash.
- **Highlights** on the painted map: the tile the party is on has an inked outline; the open tiles next to it glow softly (pulsing gold); hovering a seen tile outlines it in white and names it (placeholder text). Badges (elite "!", cleared seal) stay by the centre.
- Cleared places ([USER] 2026-10-29, the boss-skip card): a tile whose `clear_flag` is set wears the seal, and tiles `after` it open. Clicking a tile walks the whole route leg by leg (`TravelRules.route`), past cleared tiles without going in ("WALK PAST ..."); a tile still to clear stops the road. Clicking a cleared tile still goes in. A save that stands on a place from before the hex map lands at Home.
- Click a tile to walk there. The mini party walks the path in real time. Encounters are planned when the leg starts (between 20% and 85% of the way, 8 seconds apart) and shown as "?" on the path.
- On the road: HUNT, FISH, FORAGE (dice rolls, placeholders for minigames; each one once per 15 seconds walked), TALK (a party member's line, `TALK_<ID>_<n>`), PACK and LODESTONE. **FAST FORWARD** (or Tab) jumps to the next encounter, or to the end of the leg.
- Food: each journey day (30 seconds walked) ends at the night camp, where the player picks what each member eats (or a team meal); with too little food the journey is over (home, -10 morale). Nights the clock passes are owed (`travel.nights_due`) and eaten before an arrival or an encounter. See `docs/systems/morale.md`.
- Encounters: `data/travel_events.csv`, the `events.csv` format. Fights (`combat:pair_wolves`, `combat:pair_bandits`) run in `combat.tscn` on a 14 x 12 valley from `MapGen.valley` (the map maker's generator, rules V9 to V11) in the path's tile set, with the wallpaper staging and no grid, and come back to the overworld. Also found items and an abandoned house to rummage (an item, or wolves).
- Arriving shows GO IN; leaving an explore map through its way out puts the party back on the overworld at that place.
- Rules: `game/scripts/overworld/travel_rules.gd` (pure, tested in `tests/test_loop.gd`). State: `Game.travel` (`then`: the places still to walk to after this leg).

## Party, pack and levels
- The adventure party is `Game.ADVENTURE_ROSTER` (the hero, then the dog once `met_dog` is set). The hero is the lead's look on the nobody class; the smithy weapon adds attack, damage, move or range (`Game.weapon_mods`).
- Screen ([USER] 2026-10-29): on explore maps and the overworld walk the game fills the whole window above Brandon's leaf frame (`game/art/source/ui/frame_bottom.png`, scaled to the screen width; `FrameHud.use_leaf_frame`). No grey border, no party list on the left, no title along the top, no help text (`FrameHud.add_help` lines are hidden; they can come back behind a key). The frame's left square is the PACK button (placeholder text); its right panel shows the party, each member in a small square frame (pawn, name, HP numbers and bar, and in a fight the job's bar), up to 4. The screen's own buttons and lines (GO IN, HUNT, END TURN, the info and the log) float in a see-through box at the top right of the map, shown only when it has something in it; LODESTONE sits above the frame on the right. The long bar and the centre diamond are bare art for now (the Lodestone medallion goes in the diamond later). Spots are in the art's own pixels (`FrameHud.LEAF_*`). Clicks on the band don't reach the map. The overworld's meadow and mountain row run on to the window's edges; its places keep their spots above the diamond (`FrameHud.clear_rect`).
- Pack: one 4 x 3 grid of shaped items (`RunState.pack`, an `InventoryGrid`; [USER] 2026-10-29). Everything carried takes room: food is stacks of meat (10 a cell), the `medicine` count is stacks of potions (3 a cell), and finds and gear are their own shapes (rope is 1 x 2). Shapes and stack sizes are data (`data/items.csv`, columns `shape` and `stack`). `run.food` and `run.medicine` read and write those stacks, so rules code still adds and spends counts; `run.items` is what the pack holds, id -> count. Gold is a number, not in the grid. Each member's weapon sits on the member (the trinket slot is earmarked, not built).
- A pickup auto-places: it tops up open stacks, then takes the first spot that fits, turning if that helps. What still doesn't fit waits in FOUND (`run.found`, 4 x 2, not carried). As soon as the map or the walk is idle, PACK opens on its own, full ([USER] 2026-10-29): repack by hand, drop things on THROW AWAY (gone for good), or fill the dog's 2 x 2 saddlebag and press SEND HOME. The dog then leaves the party (no fights, no meals) until the party is home: a wake-up in the square (fall or GO HOME) or walking into the village. His bag is unpacked into the pack when he's back. LEAVE THE REST leaves whatever still waits. The courier is any member whose `CharacterDef.saddlebag` isn't zero (the dog's is 2 x 2).
- Belts: each member has a 2 x 1 belt (`member.belt`, an `InventoryGrid`; shapes count: one 2-cell thing or two 1-cell things, stacks as in the pack). In a fight a piece can use only what's on its own belt, and using it is its action. Food and medicine counts include the belts; meals and DRINK on the walk take from the pack first, then the belts.
- PACK (overworld and explore maps) shows the grid and every belt under it: drag to move, R or right-click to turn, drag between pack and belts to fill them; a stack dropped on the same thing tops it up; THROW AWAY bins a thing for good (`PackView` with several areas and a bin, shared with the shelved village, where the bin is SELL). No tidy button. PACK also lets the hurt EAT (1 food, +2 HP) or DRINK (a potion). Both ask who ([USER] 2026-10-30, Brandon: "hard to know who you're using it on"): a card per member under the belts shows their HP and, after EAT or DRINK or while a food or potion stack is held, what one would heal them (`TravelRules.gain`); a full member's card is greyed (FULL). Click a card, or drop the stack on it (one is used, the stack stays put); Esc cancels. Never a guess, even when only one member could gain (`TravelRules.use_on`). In fights each piece still uses only its own belt.
- Coming home (a wake-up in the square, or walking into the village; `Game.home_again`): the dog rejoins and unpacks, then every material (`kind` material in `data/items.csv`; STUFF is the placeholder, found in the crypt chests, the pass cache and the den bones) leaves the pack, the belts and FOUND for the village's count, `meta.materials` ([USER] 2026-10-29). Gear and supplies stay packed. PACK shows the village pile.
- The save keeps the layout (`meta.pack.grid`, `meta.pack.belts`, and `meta.pack.waiting`: FOUND, the half-filled saddlebag and who is away); an older save with only counts is packed fresh on load.
- Elite fights ([USER] pc-6, pc-15; player-choice step 4): a group marked `elite: true` in its map's group data fights tougher (`data/combat.csv`: 150% HP, +1 damage, +1 armor), wears an ELITE badge over its pieces, warns in its spot box ("!! ELITE ...", BACK OFF still there), pays 150% XP for the win (better loot waits on the one-more-run theme), and stays beaten once beaten. A place on the overworld with an elite still standing on its maps wears a red "!" badge on its rim. Elites belong where the party can back out or go round; none is placed yet (the designer picks, the map builder places).
- The tavern ([USER] 2026-11-01 design-bb-recruit, design-bb-menu): a `tavern` ladder in `data/restore.csv` (`tavern_1`: wood 4, metal 2, food bits 3 [AI]; settler flag `rescued_bushberry`, so it opens once he is freed; gives `tavern_open`). Its box is the `counter` thing in the `tavern` map (kind `restore`, event `tavern`). Once the step is done the same box is the counter: it lists `Game.tavern_dishes()` from `data/tavern_menu.csv` (item, price, needs: `flag:<f>`, `home:<material>` in the village pile, `char:<id>` met; empty = always) and BUY puts one in the pack for its price in gold (`Game.buy_dish`). The first menu: ration (2 gold) and jerky (7), plain food that feeds and doesn't heal, and the stew (10), a cooked meal for the night camp's team meal. Morale at the tavern comes from meals only. Screenshot: `tools/explore_shot.gd -- <png> tavern tavern`.
- Rescues ([USER] 2026-11-01 design-bb-recruit): a captor group's `loot` can hold `rescue:<id>` (Bushberry: `rescue:bushberry` on the elite ogre the map builder places on `snow_pass`). Beating the group sets `found_<id>` and `rescued_<id>`, and `Game.rescued` adds the captive to the party if there is room; with a full party he is benched and waits at home. His spot in the village square (`sq_bushberry`, event `recruit_bushberry`) shows only once `rescued_bushberry` is set, and works as his home (COME WITH ME / STAY HOME). The line floats over the party.
- The party ([USER] pc-1, pc-10; player-choice step 3): four fight, the hero and up to three companions (`party_max` in `data/levels.csv`). A companion met for the first time (the rescued dog) joins on their own while there's room. Who comes is changed only at home: going out the village gate asks GO (F) or EDIT PARTY (P), and the editor lists every companion met, coming or staying home (`meta.benched`). A companion's home in the village can use the event `companion_<id>` (COME WITH ME / STAY HOME; effects `invite:<id>` and `dismiss:<id>`, needs `party:<id>` and `noparty:<id>`); the map builder places those homes (the dog's is at village (3, 23)); a companion left home stands at their home spot, drawn by the overlay. A companion left home puts what was on their belt back in the pack. A dog sent home with a load is out until the party is home, as before.
- The weapon is the job ([USER] pc-9, pc-12, pc-13; player-choice step 2): `data/jobs.csv`. The sword makes the hero a knight (rage, PUSH HIT), the daggers a rogue (charges, DIRTY KICK, +10% crit), the bow an archer (charges, GOOD SHOT), the hidden book a mage (mana, FIRE ZAP and FIX); each job is its own class (`data/classes/job_*.tres`, the hero's base stats shifted). Before the smithy he's a nobody. The rack stays: it offers every weapon he isn't holding, so he can swap any time he's in the village (`Game.take_weapon`; the event effect `weapon:<id>`). He keeps his character level and rolled gains; his stats shift to the new job's base; each job has its own job level (on the same XP), ranks and branch, so a new job starts at 1 with no ranks and an old one comes back as it was. At job level 10 (`branch_level`) the Lodestone menu offers the job's two branches (placeholders until their skills are designed). His growth odds are his job's row in `data/growth.csv`.
- Levels and growth ([USER] pc-3, pc-7, pc-8; player-choice build step 1): `data/levels.csv`, 100 XP a level. Each level rolls every stat against the character's growth odds (`data/growth.csv`, % a level for HP, damage, armor, magic and crit), Fire Emblem style: a hit gives +1 (HP +2, crit +5%), a level where every roll misses gives the likeliest stat, and a 0 means the stat never grows (the dog never gains magic). The level-up line says what went up. Skills grow with the stats on their own (damage, and magic for spells and heals). Every 4 levels a skill rank waits in the Lodestone menu (its button glows): pick one of the character's own skills; each rank adds that rank's effect from `data/skill_ranks.csv` (BITE: a longer slow, then BLEED, then +1 damage), up to the last rank there. BLEED takes 1 HP at the start of each of the bleeding piece's phases. Rules: `LevelRules` (pure); the gains and ranks are in `meta.levels`. An older save keeps what its levels and picks gave. XP for a won fight: 10, plus 25 a foe, plus 100 for a boss, to everyone in the party. Rules: `LevelRules` (pure).
- Everything lives in meta (`meta.pack`, `meta.levels`, flags), saved on every scene change, so a restart or a wake-up keeps it.
- Restoring the village ([USER] omr-1, omr-2, omr-5, omr-12; one-more-run step 2): the materials are FOOD BITS (`ingredient`), HERB, METAL, WOOD (1 x 2) and SHINY THING (`rare`, one a cell), found in chests, caches and bones; they bank into the village pile at home. Each building has a ladder of steps in `data/restore.csv` (`needs` like `metal:3;wood:2`, `gives` like `unlock:forge_running`, the first step's `settler` flag opens the ladder). A thing of kind `restore` (its `event` names the building) shows the next step and what the pile holds toward it, GIVE THINGS when it's all there, and WATCH. Paying starts the step; it finishes the next time the party comes home from a trip (`meta.trips` counts trips out the gate), with a line on the wake-up. VILLAGE WORK in the Lodestone menu lists every ladder (done, under way, open with its needs, or locked), with WATCH on any open step. Watched steps show what they still miss in the frame's long bar (counting the pack on a trip), and a pickup that counts floats "... COUNT FOR ...". Rules: `RestoreRules` (pure); state in `meta.restore` and `meta.tracked`.
- The first ladders ([USER] omr-3, pc-14; one-more-run step 3): the SMITHY (FORGE FIRE, then SHARP STONE) and the INN KITCHEN (KITCHEN FIX, BIG POT, FULL KITCHEN; each sets a `kitchen_<n>` flag that opens recipes). Once the stone is done each weapon has its own ladder (+1 to +3, `sword_1` ...; +1 damage a level, `upgrade_damage`), and the smithy's box shows the next upgrade for the weapon in hand. A ladder still shut says why: nobody lives there yet, or FIRST NEED the step that opens it. The smith and the chef move in by placeholder events (`settler_smith`, `settler_chef`: WELCOME sets the flag) that the map builder places; the pack upgrade waits for Brandon to name its settler.
- Meals and camp ([USER] omr-9; one-more-run step 4): `data/recipes.csv` (each recipe's meal item, its ingredients, the kitchen flag that opens it, its buff). At the inn's box (`restore` thing `inn`) the open recipes show with what the village pile holds, and COOK turns the ingredients into a meal item in the pack (MEAT POT and BIG MEAT 1 x 2, BIG FEAST 2 x 2). On the road, MAKE CAMP (once a leg): the party stands still `camp_seconds` on the road clock (meals still come due, `travel.rest`), and COOK eats a carried meal: its buff (`hp:+4`, `damage:+1`, ...) lasts the next `meal_fights` fights (2), counted down at every fight's end (`Game.fight_done`). The Lodestone menu shows it (FULL BELLY). `tools/overworld_shot.gd camp` shows the camp.
- Blessings and setbacks ([USER] omr-6, omr-7, omr-8; one-more-run step 5): `data/boons.csv` holds 10 blessings and 6 setbacks (placeholder names; effects in mod keys: `damage:+1`, `defense:-1`, `attack:+2` for crit, `move`, `hp`, `magic_power`, plus `rage_per_hit`, `crit_heal`, `start_res` and `food` per meal). Road event outcomes name `blessing:any` or `setback:any` (`data/travel_events.csv`: good finds and pushing through the cold bless; a failed sneak, a bad rummage or a cold slog set back), and one is drawn at random that the party doesn't hold yet. Five blessings at most (`blessing_max`). They last until the party is home and show in the Lodestone menu.
- The bookmark ([USER] omr-4, omr-11; one-more-run step 1): `meta.bookmark` holds where the party is: the explore map and the hero's cell, or the overworld walk (mid-leg too), plus the explore memory (seen cells, used things, beaten groups). Screens write it as they open, after a fight ends, at each overworld arrival, on SAVE AND QUIT in the Lodestone menu, and when the window closes (`Game._notification`). A fight doesn't write it: the one taken when the fight began stands (it names the group, or the road encounter), so a fight quit midway starts again from its first turn (straight into the deploy). Continue on the launch menu comes back to the bookmark (`Game.continue_game`). Each write flashes SAVED at the top left. Tools and tests turn saving off (`Game.save_enabled`), so they never touch the real save.

## The Lodestone menu
Each member's level, XP, HP and fighting stats (damage, armor, crit, magic), a skill rank to spend when one waits, and GO HOME (back to the square, healed; hidden in the village itself).

## Testing it
- `tests/test_loop.gd` and `tests/test_snow_pass.gd` (in `tools/check.sh`).
- The robot: `godot --headless --path game -s res://tools/e2e_play.gd [-- seed]` plays the whole loop with fights on auto: the village steps, the dog, the statue, the road (it forages, fights what comes), the pass, the rope ledge, the den, the crypt boss, then a deliberate fall, a trip back into the crypt to check the throne stays empty, and a walk from the town gate past the cleared highlands to the dwarven mines. A lost fight wakes it in the square and it comes back to try again (up to 4 times), checking the wake-up kept everything. `E2E=1 tools/check.sh` runs it too (about 2 minutes) and fails on any SCRIPT ERROR in its output, not only on failed checks. The robot backs off from every "they're in sight" box and starts its fights with `_fight`; the explore scene runs one fight at a time (`start_fight` does nothing while one is on). Seeds 1 and 3 to 19 pass (2026-10-29; seed 2 not rerun), with 0 to 4 falls on the way. An auto fight where nobody gets hurt for a while ends as a win (the stalemate guard); the robot counts that as a failed check, not a fall.
- Foes softened ([USER] 2026-10-29, the first-fights card): wolf, bandit and bandit archer each lost 1 hit and 1 damage. With the stair melee fix, the real road fight at level 1 (combat.tscn, auto, 100 fights each) wins 98% against the wolf pair and 99% against the bandits (was 94% and 86%). Across six robot runs the Lich King is the fight that's lost (6 times), the road and the pass once or twice.
- Older numbers, by the AI-vs-AI sim on generated maps (hero with sword plus dog, before the soften): two wolves 91% at level 1; three wolves 61% at level 2 (why the den is split into 2 + 1); the Lich King alone about 46% at level 4. A player does better than the AI.
