# Exploration maps (graybox)

Code: `game/scripts/explore/`. Scene: `scenes/explore.tscn` (F6 on it, or **F10 from any screen**). Rules math is in `explore_rules.gd` (pure, tested in `tests/test_explore.gd`). Decisions are under "Exploration maps" in `docs/decisions.md`.

## How it plays
- The party leader is the one piece on the map; the rest of the party travels with them unseen. Click ground to walk there (the path previews on hover; paths take diagonals that don't cut a corner, and the leader glides at an even 5 cells a second with a hop only where the ground changes height). WASD walks freely, not cell by cell: the leader slides along walls and eases round corners (`ExploreRules.slide`, body half-width 0.26 cells), and each new cell it crosses into checks sight and enemies like a step on a path. Arrows or right drag move the camera, wheel zooms, Space snaps back to the leader. The view never turns: each map has one approved angle ([USER] 2026-10-28).
- **Staging.** Outdoor maps use the painted look's staging, as in the map maker and fights: the soft terrain pass, the set's skyline wallpaper fixed behind the view and the solid foreground band (`CombatBoard.wallpaper`). Interiors (look `plain`) keep their dark floor.
- **Fog of war is off for now** ([USER] 2026-10-28): every map draws whole and every enemy group stands in view. Spotting still works by line of sight within 7 cells (you see them first, or they see you). `ExploreRules.FOG_OF_WAR = true` brings back each map's own `fog` setting, as described next.
- **Fog of war (when on).** The leader sees 7 cells, blocked by the same line-of-sight rule as combat (walls, pillars, cliffs, trees). Never-seen cells are not drawn. Seen cells stay on the map, dimmed, once out of sight. The party can only path through cells it has seen. A map with `fog: false` (the village) shows every cell from the start.
- **Enemies.** Groups stand on the map and are drawn only while in sight. Hover one to see the cells it watches (red).
  - You see them first: the walk stops and a box asks FIGHT, SNEAK HIT (sneak check: win = the group sleeps through its first phase and the party may deploy a step further out; lose = ambush), TALK or CALM (persuade or tame: win = they let you pass), BACK OFF.
  - They see you first (you walk into their sight): ambush, the enemy moves first.
  - Other groups within 3 cells of the fight join it.
- **Fights happen in place.** The game goes turn based on the same map. First you deploy: the leader stays put; each other member gets a blue ring of cells around the leader (ring = move - 3 steps, at least 1; +1 on a sneak attack; 1 when ambushed). It starts auto-placed. Pick a member on the right, click a blue cell, ENTER or START FIGHT. Then it is the normal battle (`Battle` borrows the explore board). Win: loot from the group, downed members stand back up with 1 HP, the others fall back in with the leader. Lose: the Lodestone recalls the party.
- **Things.** Doors, rubble, chests, a hum stone, the way out. Within 3 cells a bobbing "!" icon appears; click the thing or press F to walk up and use it. A box shows the options this party has (an option can need a skill, class or character) with who rolls and the chance. Results apply to the run (food, gold, HP) and the map (door opens, stone reveals the whole map, noise draws the nearest group).

## The E2E loop (2026-10-28)
The village now also has the statue with the Lodestone, the dog's locked house with its window, and item caches; the gate needs the Lodestone and leads to the overworld; the snowy pass, wolf den and dog's house are new maps; the crypt's throne holds the Lich King. The way out of any map goes to the overworld (not the trail), and a lost fight wakes the party in the village square with everything. Won fights pay XP. The bottom bar has PACK and, once found, LODESTONE. See `docs/systems/overworld.md`.

## The starting village (PR "Starting village map")
F10 now opens `home`, the hero's cottage; the run starts inside it. Its door leads out to `village` (40 x 38, no fog): a big flat cobbled square with the well, the town hall up a terrace at the back, two homes up a bluff in the north-east (a two-high stair from the east lane), the smithy on the stream bank with the weapon rack facing the square, the tavern, five more homes, a stream with a footbridge, a ring of trees, and the gate (the way out to the trail) at the front. `house_ne` (the quiet house north-east of the square) and `tavern` are walk-in interiors; the other doors are stuck for now. No enemies. Built by `tools/explore/build_village.py`.
- **Weapon rack** (kind `rack`, event `smithy_rack`): TAKE SWORD / TAKE DAGGERS / TAKE BOW sets flag `weapon_sword` / `weapon_daggers` / `weapon_bow`; any of them removes the rack for good. The flags are placeholders for the real gear the job system adds.
- **Hidden book** (kind `hidden`, event `hidden_book`) on the bookshelf against the back wall of `house_ne`'s inner room, out of sight from the door. Taking it sets flag `found_book`. The corgi's sniff isn't in code yet.
- Buildings and furniture are placed props; the camera doesn't turn on these maps (building art is drawn from one side).

## Data
- `data/explore/<name>.json`: `grid` (same letters and height digits as sandbox maps, plus optional `props`), `start` (leader cell first), `groups` (`id`, `event`, `sight`, `loot`, `units` with class id and cell), `things` (`id`, `kind` door / rubble / chest / shrine / exit / enter / rack / hidden, `event`, `cell`, `floor` = terrain letter a door or rubble becomes when opened, `to` and `at` = the map and cell an `enter` thing leads to, `once` = flags like `a|b`: when any is set the thing is gone for good), `art_set`, `strangeness`, `view`, `turn` (false locks the camera), `fog` (false shows every cell). Doors and rubble are forced to ruin terrain while shut.
- New thing kinds for the E2E loop: `statue`, `cache`, `window`, `dog`, `climb` (drawn by `ExploreOverlay`). A thing's `when` flag hides it until the flag is set. A group's `boss` pays boss XP and `end` ends the E2E test when it is beaten.
- Option needs also take `item:<id>` (the pack holds one), `flag:<id>` and `noflag:<id>`.
- Thing kinds: `enter` with no event walks straight through to `to` (a doorway); with an event it opens the box first (`door_stuck`), and the `travel` effect goes through. `hidden` has no marker and can't be hovered or used until the leader stands next to it. Walking out of a map remembers what was seen and used there, and which enemy groups were beaten or calmed, until the party leaves through the exit (Brandon's E2E notes, 2026-10-28). Stepping into a map through a door next to a group never ambushes: the party gets the first look and the FIGHT / SNEAK HIT / CALM / BACK OFF box. Nor does a group pounce right after a choice (BACK OFF included) before the party moves.
- Placed props (`grid.props`, `CombatGrid.add_prop`): `sprite` (file stem in the cell's tile set), `cell` (back corner), `foot` (cells along x and y), `tall` (draw height in tile widths; 0 = a building that spans its footprint with its bottom on the front corner), `sight` (levels it blocks sight), `walk` (rugs). Every footprint cell is blocked unless `walk`. They draw with the footprint cell nearest the camera and fade when they hide a party piece. Buildings have their door on the left front wall (facing +y), so the door cell is the one in front of it.
- A tile set may carry `data/battle_art_<set>.csv` (same columns as `battle_art.csv`) to change which files a terrain uses there: in `village_in` rock is a plaster wall with no boulders.
- `data/explore_events.csv`: one row per event, up to 4 options. Each option: `key` (text), `need` ("" / `skill:<check>` / `class:<id>` / `char:<id>`), `check` (a trail check id or `none`), `dc`, `win`, `lose`. Effects mix run effects (`food:+3`, `hp_one:-2`, as in `events.csv`) with map effects: `open`, `remove`, `reveal`, `noise`, `exit`, `fight`, `sneak_attack`, `ambushed`, `pacify`.
- Text keys `EX_*`, `OPT_*`, `UI_EX_*` in `text/placeholder.csv` (caveman placeholders).
- The first map `crypt` (34 x 30) is generated by `tools/explore/build_crypt.py`: entry yard, a corridor with a locked door, a sealed side crypt with a chest, a sunken water channel with a raised bridge and waterfall, a cave room behind rubble with wolves, a central plaza of pillars with bandits, a graveyard with a watch tower, and a throne terrace up a grand stair with the way out.

## Headless shots
`godot --path game -s res://tools/explore_shot.gd -- out.png <step> [map]` with steps start, walk, door, spot, deploy, fight (crypt) and overview, `near:x,y`, `use:<thing id>` (any map) (needs a display; in the cloud use `xvfb-run` and `--rendering-driver opengl3`).

## Not yet
A map editor (maps are JSON or the builder script for now; the sandbox editor caps at 16 x 16), patrols and enemies that turn and search, enemies that walk toward noise instead of fighting where they stand, fleeing a fight, the trail sending the party into a map (ruins and dungeon events).

## Balance tools
- `godot --headless --path game -s res://tools/explore_balance.gd -- [fights level weapon]`: AI-vs-AI win rates for the hero and the dog against every group on the E2E maps and the road-fight pairs. Quick, but it guesses where the party stands.
- `godot --headless --path game -s res://tools/fight_probe.gd -- <map> <group> [tries]`: the real thing. Starts the map, walks the leader in from the start with the real walk, and lets the fight play on auto. This is what showed the pass wolves winning 12 of 12 from the meadow below their shelf.
