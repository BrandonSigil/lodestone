FINISH (hand-made and SIMPLE)
Match the line and paint of the style picture if one is given (the forest hike illustration), but simpler: it wins over everything else about surface.
Simplicity first: big flat shapes, few of them. Each surface is one flat colour plus at most one darker shade and 2 to 5 small hand-drawn marks. Leave most of every surface plain. No pebbles everywhere, no brick-by-brick stonework, no grass blades all over, no texture noise.
Line: one warm dark plum-grey (#373335) outline, slightly wobbly brush-pen, on silhouettes and main edges only. Inner detail lines are rare. Never black, never clean vector strokes.
Paint: flat gouache washes with a light mottle and paper grain; colour sometimes slips a little past the line. No gradients, no highlights, no airbrush.
Shapes: chunky and slightly crooked, like hand-cut wooden blocks; tops still flat and walkable. Props are simple silhouettes a child could recognise: a tree is a trunk and 2 or 3 blobs.
Colour: about 5 colours per zone, soft and earthy, faded like a printed storybook.
Avoid: detailed, intricate, busy, cluttered, fine texture, many small stones, individual bricks, individual leaves, vector art, clean cel shading, mobile game asset look, perfect geometry, glossy, bevels, gradients, high saturation.
