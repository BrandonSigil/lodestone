LODESTONE TERRAIN ZONE SHEET (exploration, many zones on one page)

Make a reference sheet: a grid of zone panels, 4 panels across and 2 down. Each panel has:
1. A label plate with the zone name.
2. One small floating island diorama of that zone, seen in a 2:1 dimetric game view (tile diamonds exactly twice as wide as tall, orthographic, no perspective). The island is about 4 by 4 tiles, built from chunky square blocks stacked in terraces of 1 to 3 steps, each step one solid slab. Show one path or stair that connects the low and high ground, and 1 or 2 big props.
3. Under it, a 5 x 5 grid of square swatches, same order in every panel:
   row 1: main ground, ground variant, path or road, hard floor, rough rock (blank tops seen straight down, no objects on them)
   row 2: water or liquid, hazard ground, second floor variant, overgrown ground, special ground (blank tops seen straight down)
   row 3: cliff side, built wall side, waterfall or liquid side, cliff side variant, hazard side (seen straight on, flat colour, 1 to 3 marks)
   row 4: small props alone on cream: bush, boulder, crate or barrel, light source, small detail
   row 5: tall props alone on cream: tree, second tree, pillar, landmark, ruin or doorway
4. No other text: only the zone name on its label plate. No title, no taglines, no captions, no swatch labels.

Style: SIMPLE, flat, graphic storybook print, like a two- or three-colour risograph or a gouache picture book. Think big flat shapes first, detail last.
- Flat colour fills: one flat colour per surface plus ONE flat shadow tone. No texture inside shapes except a light, even paper/print grain over the whole page.
- Lines: few. A single medium-weight hand-drawn outline in warm dark plum-grey (#373335) around big shapes only. No interior lines, no stone joints drawn on every block, no outline on every little leaf or pebble.
- Light: one soft sun from the upper left. Tops lightest, left sides one step darker, right sides two steps darker. That is the only shading.
- Blocks: big and few. Each terrace is one solid slab, not many small bricks. Cliff faces are one flat colour with at most 2 or 3 marks to say the material.
- Props: silhouettes. A tree is one blob of canopy on a trunk. A rock is one shape. No small leaves, cracks, grass tufts, flowers or pebbles scattered around.
- Swatches: each swatch is one flat colour with at most one simple mark or pattern hint. Lots of empty flat colour.
- Every zone uses the same block size and step height, but its OWN cliff colour and shape: root-tangled earth, cut masonry, ice shelf, layered sandstone, glassy rock and so on. Never the same grey stone under every zone.

Colour: about 4 colours per zone plus cream paper: one main family plus ONE small accent (example: volcanic = ash greys plus ember orange; crystal = slate plus violet). Soft, slightly faded, printed-book colour. No saturated red or blue anywhere else.

Images: image 1 = style picture (docs/art/references/style-flat-forest.png, Brandon's pick). Match its line, paint and level of detail exactly; it is already as simple as we want. Use its look, not its layout.
