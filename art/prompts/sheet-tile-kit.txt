LODESTONE TILE KIT SHEET (game-ready, one zone per picture)

Image 1 = the style picture (docs/art/references/style-hike-illustration.png): match its line and paint, simplified. Do NOT pass an earlier sheet as a reference: the models copy it, polish and all. Describe the zone in words instead.

Make ONE zone only: [ZONE]. Give it its own cliff material. Aspect 1:1, plain cream paper background (#eae8d6), no title, no labels, no text anywhere.
Layout: a 5 x 5 grid of equal square cells with a clear cream gap between cells. Nothing crosses a cell edge.
   row 1 (tops, straight down, seamless edge to edge, NO objects): main ground, ground variant, path or road, hard floor, rough rock
   row 2 (tops, straight down, seamless, NO objects): water or liquid, hazard ground, floor variant, overgrown ground, special ground
   row 3 (sides, straight on, exactly 4 equal horizontal stone courses filling the cell): cliff, built wall, waterfall or liquid, cliff variant, hazard side
   row 4 (one prop per cell, 2:1 dimetric 3/4 view, base centred at the bottom of the cell, cream around it): bush, boulder, crate or barrel, light source, small detail
   row 5 (one tall prop per cell, same view, base at bottom centre, may fill the cell height): tree, second tree, pillar, landmark, ruin or doorway

Rules: tops and sides are flat and seamless so they can repeat; light is even on tops and sides (the game adds the shading); props are lit from the upper left. Flat fills, a few hand-drawn marks, most of each cell plain, warm dark plum-grey outlines (#373335) on props only, never black. Muted riso colours, one zone accent, no saturated red or blue outside that accent.

Avoid: perspective on tops, shadows on tops, objects on tops, text, labels, borders around cells, 3d render, glossy, photo, characters, people, animals, watermark.

FINISH (hand-made and SIMPLE)
Match the line and paint of the style picture if one is given (the forest hike illustration), but simpler: it wins over everything else about surface.
Simplicity first: big flat shapes, few of them. Each surface is one flat colour plus at most one darker shade and 2 to 5 small hand-drawn marks. Leave most of every surface plain. No pebbles everywhere, no brick-by-brick stonework, no grass blades all over, no texture noise.
Line: one warm dark plum-grey (#373335) outline, slightly wobbly brush-pen, on silhouettes and main edges only. Inner detail lines are rare. Never black, never clean vector strokes.
Paint: flat gouache washes with a light mottle and paper grain; colour sometimes slips a little past the line. No gradients, no highlights, no airbrush.
Shapes: chunky and slightly crooked, like hand-cut wooden blocks; tops still flat and walkable. Props are simple silhouettes a child could recognise: a tree is a trunk and 2 or 3 blobs.
Colour: about 5 colours per zone, soft and earthy, faded like a printed storybook.
Avoid: detailed, intricate, busy, cluttered, fine texture, many small stones, individual bricks, individual leaves, vector art, clean cel shading, mobile game asset look, perfect geometry, glossy, bevels, gradients, high saturation.
