LODESTONE TILE KIT SHEET (game-ready, one zone per picture)

Image 1 = the style picture (docs/art/references/style-flat-forest.png, Brandon's pick): match its line, paint and level of detail exactly; it is already as simple as we want. Use its look, not its layout: it is a diorama, this picture is a grid. Do NOT pass an earlier sheet as a reference: the models copy it, polish and all. Describe the zone in words instead.

Make ONE zone only: [ZONE]. Give it its own cliff material. Aspect 1:1, plain cream paper background (#eae8d6), no title, no labels, no text anywhere.
Layout: a 5 x 5 grid of equal square cells with a clear cream gap between cells. Nothing crosses a cell edge.
   row 1 (tops, straight down, seamless edge to edge, NO objects): main ground, ground variant, path or road, hard floor, rough rock
   row 2 (tops, straight down, seamless, NO objects): water or liquid, hazard ground, floor variant, overgrown ground, special ground
   row 3 (sides, straight on, exactly 4 equal horizontal stone courses filling the cell): cliff, built wall, waterfall or liquid, cliff variant, hazard side
   row 4 (one prop per cell, 2:1 dimetric 3/4 view, base centred at the bottom of the cell, cream around it): bush, boulder, crate or barrel, light source, small detail
   row 5 (one tall prop per cell, same view, base at bottom centre, may fill the cell height): tree, second tree, pillar, landmark, ruin or doorway

Rules: tops and sides are flat and seamless so they can repeat; light is even on tops and sides (the game adds the shading); props are lit from the upper left. Outlines (#373335) on props only, never black. No moss drips, root networks, stone joints or scattered pebbles. Muted riso colours, one zone accent, no saturated red or blue outside that accent.

Avoid: perspective on tops, shadows on tops, objects on tops, text, labels, borders around cells, 3d render, glossy, photo, characters, people, animals, watermark.

FINISH (SIMPLE, flat, graphic)
SIMPLE, flat, graphic storybook print, like a two- or three-colour risograph or a gouache picture book. Think big flat shapes first, detail last.
- Flat colour fills: one flat colour per surface plus ONE flat shadow tone. No texture inside shapes except a light, even paper/print grain over the whole page.
- Lines: few. A single medium-weight hand-drawn outline in warm dark plum-grey (#373335) around big shapes only. No interior lines, no stone joints drawn on every block, no outline on every little leaf or pebble.
- Light: one soft sun from the upper left. Tops lightest, left sides one step darker, right sides two steps darker. That is the only shading.
- Blocks: big and few. Each terrace is one solid slab, not many small bricks. Cliff faces are one flat colour with at most 2 or 3 marks to say the material.
- Props: silhouettes. A tree is one blob of canopy on a trunk. A rock is one shape. No small leaves, cracks, grass tufts, flowers or pebbles scattered around.
- Tiles: each top or side is one flat colour with at most 2 or 3 simple marks. Lots of empty flat colour.
- Every zone uses the same block size and step height, but its OWN cliff colour and shape: root-tangled earth, cut masonry, ice shelf, layered sandstone, glassy rock and so on. Never the same grey stone under every zone.

Colour: about 4 colours per zone plus cream paper: one main family plus ONE small accent (example: volcanic = ash greys plus ember orange; crystal = slate plus violet). Soft, slightly faded, printed-book colour. No saturated red or blue anywhere else.
