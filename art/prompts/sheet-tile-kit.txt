LODESTONE TILE KIT SHEET (game-ready, one zone per picture)

Use this once a zone from the zone sheet is chosen. Image 1 = the chosen zone panel (look to match). Image 2 = the zone palette card if there is one.

Image 3 (optional) = the style picture (docs/art/references/style-hike-illustration.png): match its line and paint.

Make ONE zone only: [ZONE]. Aspect 1:1, plain cream paper background (#eae8d6), no title, no labels, no text anywhere.
Layout: a 5 x 5 grid of equal square cells with a clear cream gap between cells. Nothing crosses a cell edge.
   row 1 (tops, straight down, seamless edge to edge, NO objects): main ground, ground variant, path or road, hard floor, rough rock
   row 2 (tops, straight down, seamless, NO objects): water or liquid, hazard ground, floor variant, overgrown ground, special ground
   row 3 (sides, straight on, exactly 4 equal horizontal stone courses filling the cell): cliff, built wall, waterfall or liquid, cliff variant, hazard side
   row 4 (one prop per cell, 2:1 dimetric 3/4 view, base centred at the bottom of the cell, cream around it): bush, boulder, crate or barrel, light source, small detail
   row 5 (one tall prop per cell, same view, base at bottom centre, may fill the cell height): tree, second tree, pillar, landmark, ruin or doorway

Rules: tops and sides are flat and seamless so they can repeat; light is even on tops and sides (the game adds the shading); props are lit from the upper left. Flat fills, small hand-drawn marks one value step off the fill, warm dark plum-grey outlines (#373335) on props only, never black. Muted riso colours, one zone accent, no saturated red or blue outside that accent.

Avoid: perspective on tops, shadows on tops, objects on tops, text, labels, borders around cells, 3d render, glossy, photo, characters, people, animals, watermark.

FINISH (hand-made, not polished)
Match the line and paint of the style picture if one is given (the forest hike illustration): it wins over everything else about surface.
Line: thick, wobbly brush-pen ink with uneven weight, slightly broken in places, warm dark plum-grey (#373335), never black, never clean vector strokes.
Paint: flat gouache or watercolour washes with mottled, blotchy texture and visible paper grain; colour sometimes slips a little past the line; no smooth gradients, no glossy highlights, no airbrush.
Marks: short dashes and ticks for grass, cracks and stone joints, drawn by hand, a few per surface, not a pattern.
Shapes: chunky and slightly crooked, like hand-cut wooden blocks: edges not perfectly straight but tops still flat and walkable, big simple masses, few small details.
Colour: few colours per zone (about 6), soft and earthy, a bit faded like a printed storybook.
Avoid: vector art, clean digital cel shading, mobile game asset store look, perfect geometry, uniform line weight, glossy, shiny, bevels, gradients, high saturation, tiny detail everywhere.
