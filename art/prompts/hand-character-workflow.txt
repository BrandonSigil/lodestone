=== PART 1: FIXED (same every time) ===
A character animation sprite sheet for a cosy fantasy tactics game. Follow the grid template image exactly: one figure in each numbered box, sized to fit its box, standing on the diamond at the bottom of its box, and nothing at all in the crossed-out boxes. The rows are, top to bottom: IDLE 4 frames then a BLINK frame (frame 5 is frame 1 with the eyes closed); WALK 6 frames, a walk cycle in place; ATTACK 6 frames: wind up, wind up, strike, follow through, recover, settle; CAST 6 frames: gather, gather, raise, release (one small round soft spark above the raised hand), hold, settle; HURT 2 frames (flinch, recoil) then DOWN 2 frames (sitting on the ground dazed). Every frame in a row is the same size and the feet stay on the same spot, as if the frames will be played over each other.
Camera: 2:1 dimetric, a high game camera looking down about 30 degrees, orthographic, no perspective, the same in every frame. Chibi proportions, about 2.3 heads tall, a big round head and a small simple body, about 96 px tall in its 192 px box. The eyes always show: two small simple dark oval eyes with a tiny light catch-light, set wide, never hidden by hair or a hood, no bright white eye whites.
Look: a soft, slightly wobbly dark warm charcoal marker outline (#373335, never pure black) round the whole figure and its big parts, flat soft watercolour fills, one flat colour per material plus one flat shadow tone, light from the upper left, muted warm cosy colours, no gradients, no fine detail.
Background: flat solid magenta (#ff00ff) everywhere, perfectly even, no texture, no gradient, no floor, no ground shadow, no scenery. The box lines, diamonds, numbers and labels of the template must NOT appear in the picture. No text, letters, numbers or labels anywhere. Every figure fully separate, nothing touching. Nothing glows except the cast spark. Avoid: perspective, different angles between frames, extra characters, motion blur, speed lines, 3d render, photo, pure black outlines, thin hairline outlines.
