LODESTONE FALL VILLAGE BUILDING, ROOTED LOOK, PAINTED OVER A BLOCK-OUT (one building per picture, game-ready)

Image 1 = the block-out (art/templates/buildings/guide_<name>.png, from tools/art/building_guide.py). It is drawn on the game's own tile grid. Keep it EXACTLY: the same camera angle, the same footprint and outline on the ground, the same masses, wall heights, roof shapes and ridge directions, the door in the same place and at the same size. Paint the building over it; do not move, grow, shrink or add masses. The grey rounded figure beside the door is only a size guide for a person: leave it out of the picture, no people at all.
Image 2 = the finish: docs/art/references/style-flat-forest.png. Copy its soft painted fills, its line art and its amount of detail exactly.
[ZONE]

Aspect 1:1, plain cream paper background (#eae8d6), the building centred with clear cream all round it. No text, no letters, no writing on signs.

ROOTED IN THE LAND (the village look, Brandon 2026-10-29): earthy, low and solid, not a storybook village. Walls of rough rounded fieldstone in warm greys and browns, with soft rounded corners and small deep-set windows; only a little dark timber (a lintel, a door frame), never stripes of half-timbering. Heavy roofs that sit low: thick grass turf or mossy grey-green slate, or golden thatch where asked; old faded red clay tiles only where the ask names them. A bit moss covered: soft green moss on the roof, along the foundation and in the corners, a little ivy. The building looks like it grew out of the ground it sits on. Fall colours round it: golden grass, a few orange leaves. Quiet and left alone for years, but not a ruin.

GROUNDING: walls rise from the foundation. At the foot of the walls, a thin band of golden grass tufts no deeper than the foundation stones, and at most one small fall bush, all INSIDE the block-out's outline on the ground. Nothing spreads past the footprint. No ground slab, no grass pad, no path, no cast shadow.

FINISH: three flat values, light from the upper left: roof lightest, left wall mid, right wall darkest. Walls one flat stone colour with only a few soft rounded stone shapes, not every stone drawn; roof one flat colour with a few soft darker bands or moss patches, no single tiles or blades of grass drawn; moss as soft flat green patches. Windows: only as many as the ask says.

LINE ART (ESSENTIAL, the house style): every building is drawn with a clear soft, imperfect paint-marker outline, exactly like image 2, where every tree, rock and block edge has one. A dark warm charcoal line (#373335, not pure black), about as thick as the line round the trees and rock of image 2, slightly wobbly and uneven in weight, with rounded ends, like a felt marker drawn by hand. It runs round the whole silhouette and along the big inner edges: roof eaves and ridges, wall corners, chimney, foundation, door, each window. Inner lines may be a little lighter than the silhouette line.

Avoid: half-timbered walls, cream plaster walls, bright red tile roofs, storybook fantasy cottage, changing the block-out's shape or angle, a bigger or smaller door than the block-out, extra wings or storeys, grass pads spreading past the walls, ground slabs, paths, fences, crates, barrels, cast shadows, perspective, vanishing point, single roof tiles, every brick drawn, wood grain, plank lines, pixel art, dithering, 3d render, glossy, photo, people, characters, the grey guide figure, text, letters, watermark, missing or faint outlines, thin even hairline outlines, crisp vector lines, pure black outlines, saturated red, saturated blue.
