KEY ART SAMPLER for the Lodestone isometric game, v8: one hero, simple terrain, consistent scale. One picture, three panels side by side, separated by thin plum-grey rules, each panel with a narrow paper-white margin. No title, no text. Use only the hero from image 1; do not copy its floor grid or plain background.

TERRAIN AND PRINT (match exactly across all three panels): heavy hand-inked outlines in warm plum-grey #373335 with a wobbly pen edge, never pure black. Soft watercolour wash with a light paper grain. Colour sits a little off register from the outlines. Bare paper shows in pale areas. Light from the upper left: tops brightest, left faces mid, right faces darkest, one flat shadow tone per surface. One colour family per panel plus one warm accent.

TERRAIN SIMPLICITY: each panel has at most five large shapes: one platform, one arch, one column or tree, and at most two blocks. No rubble, no small stones, no leaves, no vines, no flowers, no scattered pieces, no bricks. Big calm surfaces with bare paper between them.

CAMERA: strict isometric, 2:1 dimetric, orthographic. Ground planes are exact diamonds, tile edges at 26.565 degrees. No perspective, no horizon, no sea, no sky gradient. Panels fade to bare paper at the outer edges.

SCALE (the most important rule): the hero is about 2.5 heads tall and stands about the height of one stone block on the platform. He is no taller than the nearest block beside him. He occupies roughly one twelfth of the panel height, never a third. Keep this scale in every panel.

HERO (match image 1): chibi proportions, big round head, compact body. Messy brown spiky hair with a fringe falling over one eye. Green cloak hanging from the shoulders, hood pushed back, rounded collar, ragged hem. A blue tunic vest over pale cream sleeves, a brown belt with a pouch on the right hip, brown wrist cuffs, dark grey-brown trousers, brown boots. On the back, a sword with a silver blade and wrapped brown grip, and a brown pack beside it. One thick outline per shape, one flat shadow tone per surface. Faces are blank or nearly blank, with two dots for eyes and the fringe over one.

INTEGRATION: he stands on the platform with his boots slightly sunk into the grass, one block just behind him and one tree stub just to his left, so he sits in the scene. A soft shadow falls from his feet in the same light direction as the terrain.

PANELS:
Panel 1, left, ruins: one tall column leaning slightly, one round arch with a chipped keystone, one stepped block. Coral, ochre and butter yellow with one cream accent. No figures.

Panel 2, centre, forest: the HERO, one-twelfth of the panel height, standing on a single round grass platform, between one block and one tree stub, with one arch behind. Leaf green and sage, warm orange as the only accent.

Panel 3, right, canyon: one stone bridge across a dark gorge, one pale gap of sky in the cliff. Two or three chunky cliff slabs with thick outlines. Ultramarine and cool teal, one pale accent. No figures.

AVOID: photorealism, 3D render, gradients, cel shading, glossy highlights, hairline outlines, even uniform outlines, stipple texture, hatching, rubble, small stones, leaves, vines, flowers, bricks, a copy of the floor grid or background from image 1, a pointed hood, more than one sword, extra characters beside the hero, a hero larger than one block, wide round heads, perspective, fisheye, horizon, sea, moon, neon, pure black, text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, watermarks, UI, frames with titles, speech bubbles, windows.
