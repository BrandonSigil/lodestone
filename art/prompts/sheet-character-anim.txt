LODESTONE CHARACTER ANIMATION SHEET (one facing per picture; --zones fills the facing below)

Image 1 = the character sheet (docs/art/characters/00-reference-sheet.png). Draw THIS character and nobody else: same messy brown hair, green hooded cloak with a round gold clasp, blue tunic, cream undershirt, brown belt and pouches, dark trousers, brown boots, a sword in a sheath on the back and a rolled pack. Same chibi proportions (about 2.3 heads tall), same flat colours, same simple shading. Copy the drawing style of image 1; do not add detail.

EYES (the one change from image 1, IMPORTANT): his EYES SHOW. In image 1 the fringe hides them; here the messy fringe is a little shorter and parted so both eyes are clearly visible under it in every frame where the face can be seen: two simple dark oval eyes with a tiny light catch-light, set wide on the face, readable at small size. Same hair colour and shape otherwise.

LINE ART (essential, the house style): a soft, imperfect paint-marker outline round the whole figure and its big parts (hair, face, cloak, arms, legs, boots, pack, sword), dark warm charcoal (#373335, not pure black), slightly wobbly and uneven in weight like a felt marker drawn by hand. Not a thin, even vector hairline.

FACING for every frame on this sheet: [ZONE]
Every frame shows the character from this same direction, seen from the high game camera of image 1 (2:1 dimetric, looking down about 30 degrees, no perspective).

LAYOUT: a sprite sheet on plain flat cream paper (#eae8d6). Five rows, frames evenly spaced left to right, each figure fully separate from its neighbours with clear empty cream between them. Every figure is the same size and stands on the same invisible baseline within its row, feet in the same place, as if the frames will be played over each other.
   row 1 IDLE and BLINK, 5 frames: frames 1 to 4 standing relaxed, breathing, eyes open; the cloak sways a little. Frame 5 BLINK: exactly frame 1 with the eyes closed (two short soft curved lines). If the face cannot be seen from this facing, frame 5 simply repeats frame 1.
   row 2 WALK, 6 frames: a walk cycle in place (contact, down, pass, up, then the other leg), arms swinging, cloak swinging.
   row 3 SWING, 6 frames: draws the sword over the shoulder and slashes in the facing direction: wind up, wind up, STRIKE (sword fully swung out in front), follow through, recover, back to standing with the sword out.
   row 4 CAST, 6 frames: gathers both hands, raises one hand, a small round blue spark appears above the raised hand on frame 4, holds, settles.
   row 5 HURT and DOWN, 4 frames: flinch backwards, recoil, then sitting down on the ground dazed, then the same sitting pose.

Rules: no text, no labels, no numbers, no frame borders, no grid lines, no floor, no tiles, no ground shadow, no grass, no scenery, no extra characters. Only the spark in the cast row is an effect; nothing else glows. Keep every frame clean and simple so it reads at small size.
Avoid: perspective, different camera angles between frames, a different facing in any frame, extra detail, texture, eyes hidden under the hair, thin even hairline outlines, pure black outlines, 3d render, photo, motion blur, speed lines, text.
