Image 1 = a grey block-out guide on cream paper: four plain grey boxes drawn on a strict 2:1 dimetric grid (every floor edge runs at exactly 2 across to 1 down, like a diamond tile twice as wide as tall; uprights are perfectly vertical). Paint each prop INSIDE its grey box, so that the prop's ground edges and outline follow the box's edges exactly. Do not tilt, rotate, flatten or round any edge; no edge may run at 45 degrees or look like a top-down plan view. Keep each prop in the same place and the same size as its box.

FINISH (copy the fall village look): soft flat painted fills with a little soft mottle, one flat colour plus one shadow tone per surface, light from the upper left (top lightest, left faces mid, right faces darkest). One soft dark charcoal outline (#373335, never pure black) round each whole prop only, no inner lines. Warm stone, wood brown and fall colours: moss green, ochre, faded gold. At most two or three small golden leaf tufts touching the base; no ground disc, no grass patch, no cast shadow. Big simple silhouettes.

Avoid: tilted or rotated props, 45 degree or top-down views, round or bulky blobs, true isometric (30 degree) edges, perspective, flat-on views, ground under props, cast shadows, pixel art, fine detail, text, letters, numbers, 3d render, glossy, photo, people, animals, skulls, watermark.
