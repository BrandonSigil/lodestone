KEY ART SAMPLER for the Lodestone isometric game, v7: the hero from the hero reference picture (image 1), standing inside the terrain instead of on top of it. One picture, three panels side by side, separated by thin plum-grey rules, each panel with a narrow paper-white margin. No title, no text. Do not copy the floor grid or the plain background of image 1; use only the hero from it.

TERRAIN AND PRINT (match exactly across all three panels): heavy hand-inked outlines in warm plum-grey #373335 with a wobbly pen edge, never pure black. Soft watercolour wash with a light paper grain. Colour sits a little off register from the outlines. Bare paper shows in pale areas. Light from the upper left: tops brightest, left faces mid, right faces darkest, one flat shadow tone per surface. One colour family per panel plus one warm accent. Chunky shapes with a crooked personality: leaning columns, chipped keystones, cracked blocks, bent tree stubs.

CAMERA: strict isometric, 2:1 dimetric, orthographic. Ground planes are exact diamonds, tile edges at 26.565 degrees. No perspective, no horizon, no sea, no sky gradient, no distant landscape. Panels fade to bare paper at the outer edges.

HERO (match image 1 closely): chibi proportions, about 2.5 heads tall, big round head, compact body. Messy brown spiky hair with a fringe falling over one eye. The green cloak hangs down from the shoulders with the hood pushed back, a rounded collar, and a ragged hem; the cloak is the same green as the forest. A blue tunic vest over pale cream sleeves, a brown belt with a pouch on the right hip, brown wrist cuffs, dark grey-brown trousers, and brown boots with cuffs. On the back, a sword with a silver blade and a wrapped brown grip, slung point-up, and a brown pack beside it. Drawn with the same outlines and washes as the terrain; keep one thick outline per shape and one flat shadow tone per surface. Faces are blank or nearly blank, with two dots for eyes and the fringe over one of them.

INTEGRATION (the hero must sit inside the terrain, not float on it): his boots sink slightly into the moss and grass, with a little grass and a stone chip overlapping his boot edges. The cloak's hem picks up the moss colour and a few leaf-coloured wash marks. A soft shadow falls on the terrace from his feet, on the same light direction as the terrain. He stands between the stones and the arch, not in an empty clearing: one stone block and one tree stub overlap his left side, so he is partly tucked into the scene.

PANELS:
Panel 1, left, ruins: one tall bead-topped column leaning slightly, one round arch with a chipped keystone, two stepped stone blocks, a low wall. Coral, ochre and butter yellow with one cream accent. Two or three figures, squat: one hunched with a bent staff, one with a hand on the hip, one small and round, half hidden by a block. No sword on these.

Panel 2, centre, forest: the HERO from above, front and centre, standing on a single round grass terrace, about a third of the panel height, tucked into the stones as described above. Behind him, one arch and two tree blobs on bent stubs. Leaf green and sage, with warm orange as the only accent. The terrace has the same chunky block edge and cracked stone as panel 1.

Panel 3, right, canyon: a single stone bridge across a dark gorge, one pale gap of sky in the cliff. Three figures cross: one with a long cloak dragging behind, one with a pack, one stopping at the edge to look down. Ultramarine and cool teal, one pale accent. Cliffs as chunky slabs with thick outlines and a crack or two, no foliage.

AVOID: photorealism, 3D render, gradients, cel shading, glossy highlights, clean vector gloss, hairline outlines, even uniform outlines, stipple texture on surfaces, hatching, a copy of the floor grid or plain background from image 1, a pointed hood on the hero, more than one sword, weapons on the other figures, wide round heads, fine detail on figures, perspective, fisheye, horizon, sea, moon, sky scenes, neon, pure black, text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, watermarks, UI, frames with titles, speech bubbles, flowers, bricks, windows.
