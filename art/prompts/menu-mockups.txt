===== SHARED HEAD (starts every prompt) =====
The attached board has four labelled panels. Panel 1, THE GAME, is a screenshot of our cosy isometric fantasy tactics game, with its ornate green bottom frame. Panel 2, MENU KIT, is our approved menu look: deep green mottled felt panels inside a slim pale silver and soft blue double rim, small blue diamond studs and olive leaf sprigs at the corners only. Panel 3, STYLE, is the painting style: soft flat painted shapes, gentle hand-inked outlines, a light paper grain. Panel 4, CHARACTERS, is our party, to use as small portraits.
Paint ONE polished in-game menu screen for this game, as a 16:9 screenshot, like a professional indie game with a AAA finish (think Sea of Stars, Hades, Unicorn Overlord, Slay the Spire). The game world from panel 1 shows dimmed and softly blurred behind the menu; the green bottom frame stays at the bottom. The menu itself is built from panel 2's pieces, matched exactly in colour, rim, diamonds and leaves.
Readable hierarchy: one clear title, few words, large clear numbers, icons with short labels, portraits, coloured bars with labels. Lots of breathing room. Every window has a small round X close button at its top right in the same rim style. Nothing runs off the screen.
Words: only short game labels, written clearly in a clean storybook serif, cream on green: the ones named below and nothing else. No paragraphs, no names, no made-up words, no lorem ipsum.
Avoid: walls of text, tiny text, text running off the frame, photo textures, glossy plastic bevels, 3d render, neon, pure black, sci-fi, mobile-game clutter, extra windows.
