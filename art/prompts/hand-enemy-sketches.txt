ENEMY SKETCHES, the easy way (placeholder foes; nothing here is final)

=== PROMPT A: LINEUP (8 foes, each from the front and from the back) ===
Image 1 is a style reference only. Copy its DRAWING STYLE: chunky cute board-game-piece creatures, a bold soft dark
charcoal outline, flat soft colour fills with one shadow tone, light from the upper left, cream paper. Do NOT copy the
dog, its cloak or its bags.

Draw ONLY a lineup sheet of 8 different enemy creatures for a cosy fantasy tactics game, on plain flat cream paper.
Four columns by four rows. Each creature fills two cells in its column, one above the other: on top it faces the
lower right (three-quarter front, face and eyes showing); below it faces the upper right (three-quarter back, the
back of the head and the tail or back showing, no face). Same creature, same size, same colours in both views.
Seen from a high game camera looking down about 30 degrees, no perspective.

1: a grey wolf, lean, pointed ears, long muzzle, bushy tail
2: a big brown bear, round ears, heavy and slow, twice the wolf's size
3: a wild boar, dark brown, small tusks
4: a green goo blob with two dot eyes
5: a small dark bat with big ears and wings
6: a round dark spider with red eye dots and eight bent legs
7: a big green ogre with a club and a round belly, twice a person's height
8: the big baddie: a tall hooded figure in a long dark purple robe, a small gold crown, glowing eyes in a dark hood, a staff

Every creature stands alone with clear empty cream around it. Big simple shapes, about four colours each, nothing
scary or gory, cute rather than detailed. No text, letters, numbers, labels, grid lines, borders, floor, tiles,
ground shadows or scenery.
Avoid: detail, texture, realistic fur, black outlines, gradients, perspective, 3d render, photo.
