KEY ART SAMPLER for the Lodestone isometric game, v6: the original hero, taller and slimmer than v5, drawn in the same print as the terrain. One picture, three panels side by side, separated by thin plum-grey rules, each panel with a narrow paper-white margin. No title, no text.

TERRAIN AND PRINT (match these exactly across all three panels; this is the look of the terrain): heavy hand-inked outlines in warm plum-grey #373335 with a wobbly pen edge, never pure black. Soft watercolour wash with a light paper grain. Colour sits a little off register from the outlines. Bare paper shows in pale areas. Light from the upper left: tops brightest, left faces mid, right faces darkest, one flat shadow tone per surface. One colour family per panel plus one warm accent. Chunky shapes with a crooked personality: leaning columns, chipped keystones, cracked blocks, bent tree stubs.

CAMERA: strict isometric, 2:1 dimetric, orthographic. Ground planes are exact diamonds, tile edges at 26.565 degrees. No perspective, no horizon, no sea, no sky gradient, no distant landscape. Panels fade to bare paper at the outer edges.

HERO (the original hero, drawn with the same outlines and washes as the terrain): about 2.6 heads tall, a little taller and slimmer than a squat chibi. Head about one third of his height, round but not wide, with a soft, narrower jaw. Body compact with a visible waist and short but not stubby legs. A rounded cloak hood, not a pointed one, over the shoulders in green, drawn as one soft shape with a ragged hem. Blue tunic under the cloak, with a pale shirt collar. Brown spiky hair, with three or four clear spikes at the top of the head where the hood ends. Brown boots as two short blocks. A pack on the back with two straps and a buckle, and a small belt with a pouch. No sword, no weapon. Each detail is one shape with one thick outline and one flat shadow tone; no fine lines. Faces are blank or nearly blank, with at most two dots for eyes. Seen from the iso camera above and in front, standing on the terrace with one hand at the pack strap.

PANELS:
Panel 1, left, ruins: one tall bead-topped column leaning slightly, one round arch with a chipped keystone, two stepped stone blocks, a low wall. Coral, ochre and butter yellow with one cream accent. Two or three figures, slim-to-squat: one hunched with a bent staff, one with a hand on the hip, one small and round, half hidden by a block. No sword on these.

Panel 2, centre, forest: the HERO from above, front and centre on a single round grass terrace, about a third of the panel height. Behind him, one arch and two tree blobs on bent stubs. Leaf green and sage, with warm orange as the only accent. The terrace has the same chunky block edge and cracked stone as panel 1.

Panel 3, right, canyon: a single stone bridge across a dark gorge, one pale gap of sky in the cliff. Three figures cross: one with a long cloak dragging behind, one with a pack, one stopping at the edge to look down. Ultramarine and cool teal, one pale accent. Cliffs as chunky slabs with thick outlines and a crack or two, no foliage.
