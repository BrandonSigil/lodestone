Image 1 = a snowy cliff painting. Copy its ROCK exactly: the tall cliff faces of warm grey rock, soft flat paint in faint dabs with chippy edges, a few thick dark charcoal crack lines running straight down the face, a slightly darker strip beside some cracks, broad vertical planes a shade lighter or darker. Same brushwork, same amount of detail, same soft hand-painted feel. Do NOT copy its snow, its water, its trees, its stairs or its camera angle.

WHAT THIS IS: cliff-face strips for a game. The game cuts these strips out and places them side by side along every cliff, each one starting at a random height and repeating downward as far as the cliff goes, so cliffs of any height look like one hand-painted face with no breaks. Each strip is a flat FRONT VIEW of a tall band of rock cliff, seen perfectly straight on: a plain tall rectangle of rock, no top surface, no grass, no snow, no ground, no sky, no perspective, no corner.

LAYOUT: aspect 3:2, plain flat cream paper (#eae8d6) background, no title, no labels, no numbers, no text. SIX TALL STRIPS side by side in one row, each about three times taller than wide, all the same size, with a clear cream gap between them and cream above and below; nothing crosses into another strip. The six strips are six different versions of the same rock: the cracks and planes in different places, so no two look alike.

SEAMLESS TOP TO BOTTOM (essential): the bottom edge of every strip continues exactly into its own top edge, so the strip can repeat downward forever with no join: every crack, plane and dab that leaves the bottom comes back in at the top. No horizontal line, ledge, band or change of tone anywhere across a strip.
SIDES: the left and right edges of every strip are plain rock in the middle tone, with no crack touching the side edges, so any strip can sit beside any other with no visible join.
Inside a strip: one or two thick dark cracks at most, running the whole height, at different positions in each version; faint dabs everywhere; the light is flat and even, never coming from the left or the right side, and even from top to bottom (the game shades the faces).
Colour: warm grey rock like image 1 (about #8c827d for the middle tone, a little lighter and darker for the planes), dark warm charcoal cracks (#373335, never pure black).

Avoid: light from one side, cast shadows, snow, grass, moss, ground, sky, water, borders or frames around the strips, outlines round the strips, cracks touching the left or right edge, horizontal bands, ledges, lines across, bricks, blocks, strata, perspective, isometric blocks, top surfaces, corners, gradients from top to bottom, pixel art, 3d render, photo, text.
