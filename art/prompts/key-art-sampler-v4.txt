KEY ART SAMPLER for the Lodestone isometric game, v4: the v3 look, with squatter characters that have more personality. One picture, three panels side by side, separated by thin plum-grey rules, each panel with a narrow paper-white margin. Same camera and same print style in all three. No title, no text.

KEEP FROM v3: heavy hand-inked outlines in warm plum-grey #373335, wobbly pen edge, never pure black. Soft watercolour wash with a light paper grain. Colour sits a little off register from the outlines. Bare paper shows in pale areas. One value per surface plus one shadow tone. One colour family per panel plus one warm accent. Simple, chunky shapes. Objects with a crooked personality: a leaning column, a chipped keystone, cracked blocks, bent tree stubs.

CAMERA: strict isometric, 2:1 dimetric, orthographic. Ground planes are exact diamonds, tile edges at 26.565 degrees. No perspective, no horizon, no sea, no sky gradient, no distant landscape. Panels fade to bare paper at the outer edges. Light from the upper left: tops brightest, left faces mid, right faces darkest.

CHARACTERS (new in v4): squat and chunky, built for the isometric camera. Take a pinch of the chibi key art: a big round head and a small, compact body, roughly two heads tall, not a slim adult. The head is about one third of the figure's height. Legs are short and stubby. Shoulders and cloaks are wide, so the silhouette reads as a rounded lump from above. Personality comes from posture and one quirk per figure, drawn in thick outline: a hunched shoulder, a hand on the hip, a tilted hat, a scarf that blows sideways, a wide stance, a bent staff. Each figure must read as a different person at a glance. Faces stay blank or nearly blank, with at most two dots for eyes. No fine detail, no buttons, no strands of hair, no sword.

PANELS:
Panel 1, left, ruins: one tall bead-topped column leaning slightly, one round arch with a chipped keystone, two stepped stone blocks, a low wall. Coral, ochre and butter yellow with one cream accent. Two or three squat figures: one with a bent staff and a hunched shoulder, one with a hand on the hip, one small and round, half hidden by a block.

Panel 2, centre, forest: the HERO stands front and centre on a single round grass terrace, about a third of the panel height. Behind him, one arch and two tree blobs on bent stubs. Leaf green and sage, with warm orange as the only accent. HERO: squat and chunky, big round head, small compact body, about two heads tall. A hooded green cloak drawn as one wide bell with a thick outline and a ragged hem, a blue tunic as one rectangle, a pale shirt collar, brown spiky hair as one dark cap with three spikes, brown boots as two stubby blocks, a small square pack on the back, slightly tilted. Stance relaxed, one hand near the pack strap. No sword, no face detail. Seen from the iso camera above and in front.

Panel 3, right, canyon: a single stone bridge across a dark gorge, one pale gap of sky in the cliff. Three squat figures cross it: one with a long cloak dragging behind, one with a pack, one stopping at the edge to look down. Ultramarine and cool teal, one pale accent. Cliffs as chunky slabs with thick outlines and a crack or two, no foliage.

AVOID: slim or tall adult proportions, chibi-only big-head heads that lose the squat body, fine detail, thin hairline outlines, even uniform outlines, clean vector gloss, gradients, cel shading, stipple texture on surfaces, hatching, photorealism, 3D render, perspective, fisheye, horizon, sea, moon, sky scenes, neon, pure black, text, letters, numbers, labels, logos, watermarks, UI, frames with titles, speech bubbles, vines, leaves, flowers, bricks, windows, a sword, a hood on the hero's head.
