THE SEVEN THINGS IN IMAGE 1. Top row left to right, the first three each a single stretch of wall one floor tile long and about two people tall, seen from the game camera, its long face turned down-left toward the viewer and lit, its thin end and top edge showing:
   1 PLAIN WALL.  2 WALL WITH A WINDOW (the dark opening).  3 WALL WITH A DOORWAY (the dark opening, standing on the bottom edge).  4 CORNER POST: a single slim square post a little taller than the walls, standing where two walls meet in the back corner of a room. Only the post; no walls on it.
Bottom row: 5 FLOOR and 6 WORN FLOOR, each seen STRAIGHT DOWN from above, filling its square edge to edge, repeating in every direction with no seam.  7 FLOOR EDGE, seen STRAIGHT ON, filling its square edge to edge, repeating left to right with no seam: what the side of the floor looks like along the room's open front.

Plain cream paper (#eae8d6) round everything, exactly as in image 1. Nothing else in the picture: no floor under the walls, no ground, no furniture, no props, no people, no cast shadows. No text, no labels, no numbers.

FINISH: three flat values, light from the upper left: wall tops lightest, the long lit faces mid, the shaded faces and thin ends darkest. One flat colour per material with only a few big soft shapes to say what it is, most of each face plain. The window and doorway openings stay dark (the doorway may show a little dark floor beyond).

LINE ART (essential, the house style): a clear soft, imperfect paint-marker outline round every wall piece and along its big inner edges (top edge, ends, frames, posts, beams), exactly like image 3. Dark warm charcoal (#373335, not pure black), slightly wobbly and uneven in weight, rounded ends, like a felt marker drawn by hand. On the three floor squares only the inner joints get lines, never the square's outer edge.

Avoid: changing image 1's shapes, angle or sizes, extra wall pieces, perspective, vanishing point, room views, furniture, props, people, floor under the walls, cast shadows, light beams, gradients, pixel art, dithering, every brick or plank grain drawn, wood grain lines, texture noise, glossy, 3d render, photo, text, letters, labels, borders, pure black lines, thin even hairlines, saturated red, saturated blue.
