LODESTONE WALL KIT (flat wall and floor pieces for room back walls, one kit per picture; --zones fills THIS KIT)

Image 1 = Brandon's target room (docs/art/references/interior-walls-target.webp). Take from it WHAT the walls and floor are made of and how they are put together: the plaster, the timber posts and beams, the small window, the doorway, the plank floor. Do NOT copy its pixel-art finish, its dark blue background, its camera angle or its furniture.
Image 2 = the finish: docs/art/references/style-flat-forest.png. Copy its soft flat painted fills, its paint-marker line art and its small amount of detail exactly.
[ZONE]

WHAT THESE ARE: flat texture pieces that the game wraps onto its own wall faces and floor tops, like wallpaper. So every wall piece is drawn STRAIGHT ON, as a flat elevation seen square from the front (no angle, no depth, no perspective, no corner turning), and every floor piece is drawn STRAIGHT DOWN from above. The game turns them to its camera, shades the two wall directions itself and adds the corners.
SCALE (the same in every cell): one square cell = a stretch of wall two floor tiles wide and four height steps tall, where a person stands about three steps tall. A window or doorway sits centred left to right in its square and wholly inside it: a doorway about nine tenths of the square tall and four tenths of it wide, standing on the bottom edge; a small window about a quarter of the square wide, a little above the middle.

LAYOUT: aspect 1:1, plain cream paper (#eae8d6). A 3 x 3 grid of equal square cells with a clear cream gap between cells; each piece fills its whole cell edge to edge, square corners, nothing crosses into the gap. No title, no labels, no numbers, no text anywhere.

SEAMLESS: the plain wall pieces repeat left to right and top to bottom with no seam; the floor tops repeat in every direction; the floor edge and the wall-foot band repeat left to right. Pieces with an opening (window, doorway) have plain wall at their left and right edges, so they sit in a row of plain wall with no seam.

FINISH: flat even light on every piece (the game shades the faces); one flat colour per material with only a few big soft shapes to say what it is (a few soft worn patches on plaster, a few soft stone shapes, a few plank joints), most of each piece plain. LINE ART (the house style): a soft, imperfect paint-marker line, dark warm charcoal (#373335, not pure black), slightly wobbly, on the shapes INSIDE a piece (timber edges, window and door frames, plank joints, block joints), never drawn along a cell's outer edge.

Avoid: perspective, angled or 3/4 views, corners, room views, furniture, props, people, shadows, light beams, gradients, pixel art, dithering, every brick or plank grain drawn, wood grain lines, texture noise, glossy, 3d render, photo, text, letters, labels, borders round the cells, pure black lines, thin even hairlines, saturated red, saturated blue.
